# Learn & Play ⭐

A flash-card learning game for Pre-K through 3rd grade, built as an installable iPhone/iPad web app (no App Store needed).

**Grade setting:** ⚙️ → Grade (Pre-K, Kindergarten, 1st, 2nd, 3rd). Each grade shows the games that fit it (e.g. Multiply/Divide from 2nd) and starts every game at that grade's level. **Levels follow her progress within the selected grade:** a strong round increases difficulty up to that grade's maximum and a hard round offers easier practice within the grade. A grown-up can enable Show all games to explore other levels. Turn on **Show all games** to see everything. The grade-to-section ranges, learning objectives and question routes live in `data/curriculum-map.json`.

**🎚️ Difficulty slider (grown-ups):** on any question screen, **press and hold** the 🎚️ Lv button for a second to open a slider (Easier ↔ Harder) that shows which grade each level suits.

## 🗺️ Today's Quest
The first thing on the home screen: a short guided mix for today (about 10–12 minutes), made for each child from their grade, the skill that needs the most practice, and what they haven't done lately — a warm-up, practice, reading, math, a discovery topic (and 3 letters of tracing for Pre-K/K). Each step is 5 cards; finished steps get a ✓. When all are done the 🎁 gift opens: 5 stars, a new sticker and a Play Zone break. A new quest is made every day. Everything else stays under **Explore everything**.

**How it plays:** 10 cards per round. Pictures/clues stay hidden behind **💡 Hint** and appear only when she taps it or after a wrong answer. Right answers get a spoken "Excellent!" / "Awesome job!", confetti and a bouncing emoji; 3 in a row shows a streak. Each finished round earns stars and a new sticker. Each game has 3 levels (math games have 5) and moves up automatically after a great round (grown-ups can change levels in ⚙️).

**Reading hints** break words into syllables (al · li · ga · tor, in colors, read aloud slowly); one-syllable words split into sound chunks (c · at, fr · og). Sentence hints show every long word broken into syllables while the voice reads along.

## 📚 Story Time (Pre-K and K)
A library of picture books read aloud. Each page has a hand-painted picture and a few large lines; **Read to me** plays the recorded narration and lights up each word as it is read, and a child can tap any word to hear it. Three picture questions follow each book (no reading needed), and finished books count as a learning day on the progress page. The first book is a gentle *Little Red Riding Hood* (nobody is eaten: Grandma hides and the woodcutter chases the wolf away). Pictures are painted from code in `scripts/library/` and rendered once to images with `node scripts/library/render-book.cjs red-riding-hood`; any page can be replaced by a real painting with the same file name.

## 🧠 Brain Games (always available)

Open **Brain Games** on Home for Memory Match, Picture Puzzle and Sudoku (each from the grade where it fits). These problem-solving challenges are **not locked behind earned play breaks**. The available games depend on age/grade (Pre-K: Memory, Tic-Tac-Toe, Picture Puzzle; Kindergarten also Sudoku). In Settings, **Show all games** overrides grade visibility. A completed brain puzzle is counted separately in the parent Progress dashboard.

Pattern recognition, Odd One Out and What Comes Next? are **education lessons**, found under **Thinking Skills** in the learning catalog, and included in alternating daily quests.

## 🎮 Play Zone (reward games)
The Play Zone contains arcade and creativity activities (Tic-Tac-Toe, Four in a Row, Finger Maze, Balloons, Coloring, Rolling Ball 3D, Pinball, Ball Maze, Tap the Mole, Draw & Copy, Silly Moves). Brain Games listed below are available separately on Home. Two rules to choose from in ⚙️ → Play time rule: **Earn by rounds** (default: every 2 finished learning rounds earn a 3-minute break) or **Minutes every hour** (free play for up to 15 minutes in each clock hour — only time actually spent playing counts). Or set "Always open". Every game shows its **level** (badge on the tile) and winning moves her up. A timer shows the time left; when it ends she is sent back to learning (coloring is saved).

## Children (profiles)
Each child has their own name, picture, grade, levels, stars, stickers, colored pictures, drawings, traced letters and Play Zone time. Tap the picture at the top of the home screen to switch (with more than one child the app also asks **Who is playing?** when it opens). Add, rename, change the picture, or remove a child in ⚙️ → Children (up to 6). Voice, PIN and the Play Zone rules are shared by the whole device. Older saved progress becomes the first child automatically.

## Grown-up PIN
⚙️ asks for a 4-digit PIN (you make one the first time). Forgot it? Tap **Forgot PIN?** and answer a grown-up multiplication question to make a new one. Change it any time in ⚙️.

## Backup
⚙️ → **Save a backup** downloads a small file with all progress; **Load a backup** brings it back (on this or another device).

## Hidden test mode
In ⚙️, **tap the "Grown-up Settings" title 5 times** to show 🧪 Test mode: the right answer is outlined, ⏮ ⏭ step back and forward through cards, 🏁 ends the round, every game is shown, the Play Zone is always open, and a line shows voice clips / 3D support / screen size. The **Question browser** opens any game at any level with 30 cards to flip through (it never changes her levels or stars).

## Install on iPhone / iPad
1. Open https://sudiptoimpmail.github.io/kids-learn/ in **Safari**.
2. Tap **Share** → **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Open it from the home screen — it runs full-screen and works offline.

## Voice
Every word, question and praise is pre-recorded in a natural voice (`audio/`, made with the open-source Kokoro TTS model, voice `af_heart`), so it sounds the same on every device and works offline.
If a phrase has no recording, the app falls back to the best voice installed on the device. For a better fallback on iPhone/iPad: **Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → English → download "Ava (Premium)" or "Zoe (Premium)"**.

## Edit content
All bundled questions, stories, vocabulary, geography and drawing geometry now live in versioned `data/*.json` files. Game logic remains in JavaScript. Photography, illustration, currency, workbook and landmark media are stored as individual files in `assets/`. `content-loader.js` loads the JSON before any engine starts, and the service worker precaches it for offline use. Update `sw.js` version when changing the bundled files, and run `node tests/content-assets-test.js` before release.

## Assets and cost policy
All bundled learning icons have been replaced with locally authored SVG wrappers using system-provided emoji glyphs. The 47 coloring subjects and 39 landmark schematic illustrations are original geometric drawings. The 2D Body Parts and anatomy illustrations are local original media. There are no third-party CC BY / CC BY-SA illustrations or anatomy meshes included in this release.

**No stock-art fees, royalties, API image dependencies, or runtime downloads are needed to play.** The remaining JavaScript libraries and map/audio dependencies have their own permissive/public-data licensing terms. Those are software/data obligations, not stock-image license fees. See the asset provenance audit and retain legal notices for open-source code.

Progress is stored only on the device (localStorage). No accounts, ads or tracking.

## Offline-first optional learning packs

All bundled games and questions remain free to play **without internet, ads, or purchase prompts**. The app includes a validated, locally persisted question-pack format for future optional curriculum expansions. An already installed extra pack works offline; new packs can be obtained only through the protected parent Settings area once a real native StoreKit/Google Billing adapter and verified download service are configured. The web build does not sell or unlock anything.

Bundled question banks are JSON files and local media assets, while JavaScript handles activity generation and interaction. Optional extra multiple-choice packs are versioned JSON stored in IndexedDB. See [docs/offline-content-packs.md](docs/offline-content-packs.md) for the schema, approval constraints, parental gate, entitlement verification, and native art/audio download plan.

## Grade-to-skill routing

[`data/curriculum-map.json`](data/curriculum-map.json) is the source of truth for grade visibility, section levels, skill goals and the intended grade for each authored Grade 2–3 extension question. Grade 2 and Grade 3 do not inherit each other's new question banks solely because they share a numeric level. Authored text and routes are offline JSON; the JavaScript generators remain implementation logic.

## Shared engine: kids-core

Learn & Play and [Palabritas](https://github.com/sudiptoimpmail/palabritas) run on the same engine, **kids-core** (a separate, versioned repo). Each app keeps a copy in `core/`. Don't edit that folder here: change kids-core, then run `node ../kids-core/tools/sync-core.mjs .`, bump `sw.js` VERSION and run the tests.

- `app.config.json` holds everything app-specific that isn't code: storage key, voices, the grown-up PIN (shared with Palabritas), content packs and **colours** (`theme`).
- `core/` holds saved data, the PIN, voice, sounds, confetti, adaptive levels, content packs (schema 1) and offline support.
- The rest of this repo is Learn & Play's own games, questions and cards.
