# Learn & Play ⭐

A flash-card learning game for Pre-K through 3rd grade, built as an installable iPhone/iPad web app (no App Store needed).

**Grade setting:** ⚙️ → Grade (Pre-K, Kindergarten, 1st, 2nd, 3rd). Each grade shows the games that fit it (e.g. Multiply/Divide from 2nd) and starts every game at that grade's level. **Levels follow her progress:** a great round moves her up; at the top of her grade, two great rounds in a row move her past it; a hard round moves her back down (even below her grade). Turn on **Show all games** to see everything. The ranges live in `GRADES` in `content.js`.

**🎚️ Difficulty slider (grown-ups):** on any question screen, **press and hold** the 🎚️ Lv button for a second to open a slider (Easier ↔ Harder) that shows which grade each level suits.

## 🗺️ Today's Quest
The first thing on the home screen: a short guided mix for today (about 10–12 minutes), made for each child from their grade, the skill that needs the most practice, and what they haven't done lately — a warm-up, practice, reading, math, a discovery topic (and 3 letters of tracing for Pre-K/K). Each step is 5 cards; finished steps get a ✓. When all are done the 🎁 gift opens: 5 stars, a new sticker and a Play Zone break. A new quest is made every day. Everything else stays under **Explore everything**.

**How it plays:** 10 cards per round. Pictures/clues stay hidden behind **💡 Hint** and appear only when she taps it or after a wrong answer. Right answers get a spoken "Excellent!" / "Awesome job!", confetti and a bouncing emoji; 3 in a row shows a streak. Each finished round earns stars and a new sticker. Each game has 3 levels (math games have 5) and moves up automatically after a great round (grown-ups can change levels in ⚙️).

**Reading hints** break words into syllables (al · li · ga · tor, in colors, read aloud slowly); one-syllable words split into sound chunks (c · at, fr · og). Sentence hints show every long word broken into syllables while the voice reads along.

## 🎮 Play Zone (reward games)
Two rules to choose from in ⚙️ → Play time rule: **Earn by rounds** (default: every 2 finished learning rounds earn a 3-minute break) or **Minutes every hour** (free play for up to 15 minutes in each clock hour — only time actually spent playing counts). Or set "Always open". Every game shows its **level** (badge on the tile) and winning moves her up. A timer shows the time left; when it ends she is sent back to learning (coloring is saved).

## Looks

## Children (profiles)
Each child has their own name, picture, grade, levels, stars, stickers, colored pictures, drawings, traced letters and Play Zone time. Tap the picture at the top of the home screen to switch (with more than one child the app also asks **Who is playing?** when it opens). Add, rename, change the picture, or remove a child in ⚙️ → Children (up to 6). Voice, PIN and the Play Zone rules are shared by the whole device. Older saved progress becomes the first child automatically.

## Grown-up PIN
⚙️ asks for a 4-digit PIN (you make one the first time). Forgot it? Tap **Forgot PIN?** and answer a grown-up multiplication question to make a new one. Change it any time in ⚙️.

## Backup
⚙️ → **Save a backup** downloads a small file with all progress; **Load a backup** brings it back (on this or another device).

## Hidden test mode
In ⚙️, **tap the "Grown-up Settings" title 5 times** to show 🧪 Test mode: the right answer is outlined, ⏮ ⏭ step back and forward through cards, 🏁 ends the round, every game is shown, the Play Zone is always open, and a line shows voice clips / 3D support / screen size. The **Question browser** opens any game at any level with 30 cards to flip through (it never changes her levels or stars).

## Install on iPhone / iPad
1. Open https://sudiptoimpmail.github.io/kids-learn/ in **Safari**.
2. Tap **Share** → **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Open it from the home screen — it runs full-screen and works offline.

## Voice
Every word, question and praise is pre-recorded in a natural voice (`audio/`, made with the open-source Kokoro TTS model, voice `af_heart`), so it sounds the same on every device and works offline.
If a phrase has no recording, the app falls back to the best voice installed on the device. For a better fallback on iPhone/iPad: **Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → English → download "Ava (Premium)" or "Zoe (Premium)"**.

## Edit content
All words, sentences, vocabulary (`VOCAB`), jobs, flags, etc. live in `content.js` — add rows to the arrays. After changing files, bump `VERSION` in `sw.js` so installed copies update.
