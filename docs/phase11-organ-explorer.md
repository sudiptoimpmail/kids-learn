# Phase 11 — Explore Inside My Body

Adds an independent organ-exploration activity to existing parent-opt-in Inside My Body organ identification cards. Children can choose among eight organs (brain, heart, lungs, stomach, intestines, liver, kidneys, spine) to see a short child-friendly explanation and a highlighted location on the offline SVG. Selection and clearing use native buttons, `aria-pressed`, and live output; they do not submit answers, grant stars, or change quiz progress.

The feature is entirely local: no medical data, external images or network calls. Browser tests check all eight facts, highlighting, reset and four viewport widths. This remains a simplified anatomical illustration, not a medically reviewed 3D model or photograph. More accurate image assets and real-device VoiceOver checks remain outstanding.
